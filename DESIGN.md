# BokBac Design System

## Visual Direction

Dark clinical workstation with Liquid Glass panels, compact navigation, subdued scientific glow, and dense task surfaces. The target is the old v3 look shown in the June 10, 2026 screenshots.

## Theme

Scene: A student or educator reviews bacterial workup steps on a laptop in a dim classroom or lab after specimen processing, needing low-glare focus and quick scanning. Dark mode is the default.

## Color

- Background: near-black blue-violet, never pure black.
- Surface: translucent ink panels with subtle violet and green tint.
- Primary accent: violet for active navigation, primary actions, focus, and selected controls.
- Secondary accents: muted gold and cyan only for borders, metadata, and category contrast.
- Semantic colors: emerald success, amber warning, rose critical.

## Typography

- UI type: Inter and Prompt for Thai/English interface labels.
- Brand wordmark and major login title may use Cormorant Garamond to match v3 premium diagnostic identity.
- Keep product UI labels compact and predictable.

## Components

- Top navigation is full-width, sticky, and dense, with a thin multicolor accent rule.
- Login is standalone and centered without the app navigation.
- Page containers should use wide workstation layouts where useful, not narrow centered SaaS panels.
- Specimen guide uses a persistent left selector on desktop and detail workspace on the right.
- Library and reference pages prefer dense rows/tables over marketing cards.
- Empty states use quiet dashed glass panels.

## Motion

Use short stateful transitions only: hover, focus, reveal, and active selection. Respect reduced motion. Avoid long page-load choreography.
